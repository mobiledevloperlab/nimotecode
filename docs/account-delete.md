# Account & Data Deletion

<p class="trust-meta">Last updated: 2026-11-05 · NimoteCode</p>

Delete your account in the app, or contact support directly from this page if you cannot sign in or have uninstalled. Reinstallation is not required.

<div class="trust-summary">

**Account deletion does not cancel Apple / Google subscriptions.** Cancel separately if you do not want renewal. You need not wait for subscription expiry to request deletion.

[Apple subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) · [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)

</div>

## Delete in the app {#in-app}

1. Open NimoteCode **Settings → Delete account**.
2. Read the confirmation and type `DELETE`.
3. If prompted, sign in again or complete authentication, then confirm.

Successful completion removes the authentication account and primary user record and releases backend subscription ownership. If an error occurs or no success confirmation appears, contact support to check the outcome.

## Request from this page {#request}

<a class="trust-action" href="mailto:support@nimotecode.com?subject=NimoteCode%20account%20and%20data%20deletion">Email an account and data deletion request →</a>

Email: [support@nimotecode.com](mailto:support@nimotecode.com). If there is no default mail app, copy the address into your email service. The button opens a draft; **send the email to submit your request**.

State that you want to delete your NimoteCode account and associated personal data. Include the account email if available, sign-in method and platform (iOS / Android). For Apple Hide My Email, the relay address can help. If you cannot identify the account or used guest features, explain this so we can help establish the necessary verification.

We may verify ownership through the existing account or email. Never send passwords, one-time codes, private keys or full payment details. Do not submit deletion requests through public GitHub Issues.

## Scope and exceptions {#scope}

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Firebase authentication account and primary user record | Removed on successful account deletion. |
| Backend subscription ownership | Current ownership is released; transaction, duplicate-claim prevention and deletion linkage records may remain, including a former user identifier. |
| Device trial records | Anti-abuse hashes and trial-use records may remain. Deletion does not reset trial eligibility. |
| RevenueCat, analytics and support records | Not automatically all erased with account deletion. You may explicitly request handling of this associated data, subject to applicable obligations and necessary retention. |
| Local history, reports, credentials, workspaces, remote files and external-agent records | Not automatically removed. Manage them in the app, device, server or relevant service. |
| Store accounts, subscriptions and your AI / GitHub accounts | Not deleted or canceled; manage them with the respective service. |

You can disable automatic AI history saving, delete history, clear reports and remove AI configurations or saved SSH connections. Removing a token from the device is separate from revoking it with its provider.

## Progress and timing {#timing}

The app shows the result when its automatic flow completes. Email requests normally receive an initial response in 2–3 business days. After verification, we will explain scope, expected completion and the outcome. Additional data handling may take longer; applicable legal deadlines take precedence. If records must be retained, we will explain their scope, reason and applicable retention arrangements.

You may also request deletion of specific personal data while keeping your account; specify that scope in your email. See [Privacy](/privacy#retention) and [Support](/support).
