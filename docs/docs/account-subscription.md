# Account and subscription

## View account status

Open **Settings** to sign in and view the account and subscription section. The displayed entitlement determines access to Pro features. Availability and purchase options depend on the store, account, region, and release channel.

**Try Pro free for 14 days** — Claim your trial in the app. After 14 days, you return to Free unless you have a paid subscription or other valid Pro access, such as Early Access. Trial eligibility is shown in the app.

## Restore a purchase

If a previous purchase is not recognized, confirm the platform-store account and select **Restore Purchase**. Check the resulting entitlement before retrying the feature. For account-specific issues, contact [Support](/support).

## Feature access

In 2.5.0, Free users can browse GitHub, see the Docker version, container and image overview over supported SSH connections, and manage breakpoints. Pro is required to clone or write through GitHub, manage Docker resources, or start a debug session. Badges appear on the affected actions.

In NimoteCode 2.5.0, creating or resuming a [persistent SSH terminal](/docs/terminal#persistent-ssh-terminal) requires Pro and a compatible host with tmux installed. Native desktop builds enable Pro by default.

Mobile Nimote Connect’s managed direct and relay connections require Pro. Standard SSH, personal Tailscale, and Nimote Connect in desktop releases are free.

See [Free and Pro](/pro).

AI models use BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). Add your own provider API key; model usage is billed separately by your provider. No model credits are included.
