# Account and subscription

## View account status

Open **Settings** to sign in and view the account and subscription section. The displayed entitlement determines access to Pro features. Availability and purchase options depend on the store, account, region, and release channel.

## Restore a purchase

If a previous purchase is not recognized, confirm the platform-store account and select **Restore Purchase**. Check the resulting entitlement before retrying the feature. For account-specific issues, contact [Support](/support).

## Feature access

Some capabilities may require Pro access, depending on platform, app version, release channel, store region, and account entitlement. The app shows the access state for your account at the relevant operation. Model-provider usage is billed separately by your provider. See [Free and Pro](/pro) for the current comparison.
