# Account and subscription

## View account status

Open **Settings** to sign in and view the account and subscription section. The displayed entitlement determines access to Pro features. Availability and purchase options depend on the store, account, region, and release channel.

**Try Pro free for 14 days** — Claim your trial in the app. After 14 days, you return to Free unless you have a paid subscription or other valid Pro access, such as Early Access. Trial eligibility is shown in the app.

## Restore a purchase

If a previous purchase is not recognized, confirm the platform-store account and select **Restore Purchase**. Check the resulting entitlement before retrying the feature. For account-specific issues, contact [Support](/support).

## Feature access

The limits below follow the upcoming 2.5.0 comparison. Check your installed version and [Free and Pro](/pro) for account-specific access.

Free includes basic Git, including fetch, push, pull, and creating or switching branches, as well as GitHub cloning and basic debugging. Pro includes branch rename and deletion, stashes, manual merge and rebase, Git AI, advanced GitHub collaboration, Docker and Dev Containers, and debugger expressions and watches. Compatible tools and permissions apply.

NimoteHost, persistent remote terminals and cross-device AI session takeover require an active Pro entitlement, including a valid trial or Early Access grant.

On Android and iOS, managed Nimote Connect internet routing requires Pro and a paired computer with remote access enabled. Direct SSH and personal Tailscale remain available separately.

See [Free and Pro](/pro).

AI models use BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). Add your own provider API key; model usage is billed separately by your provider. No model credits are included.
